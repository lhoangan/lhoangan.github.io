In the midst of all the fatigue, challenges, concerns, axieties,
Bishop Barron urges everyone to go back to the first moment of the call, first heard
of the call to become. Find a time of enormous grace, inspiration, and spritual clarity
Find this moment, time is decively important for the success we do now

Appealing to very classical philosophical principles: three transcendential properties
of beings: wherever you find existence, you'll find the 3 things. Whatever is,
in the measure that it is, is true, beautiful and good. Truth, goodness and beauty
trancendential properties of being.

Truth: Being is knowable, everything is appealing to the mind, in the measure that it is
Beauty: integrity, harmony, and radiance, appealing to the soul
Good: appealing to the will, desire, call us forth, the longing of the heart

All of cultures are predicated upon the three transcendentals

Philosophers, researchers, scientists, journalists investigating journals: passionatble
about the truth, want to know, discover and propagate the truth

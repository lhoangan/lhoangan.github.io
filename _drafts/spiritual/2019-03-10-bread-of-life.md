Today I learn about Our Father prayers. What we already knew is "daily bread"
means all sort of our essence like food itself, or all other aspect like
love, family, jobs, etc.

But the homily from Bishop Barron shows a different and new aspect:


τὸν ἄρτον ἡμῶν τὸν ἐπιούσιον δὸς ἡμῖν σήμερον·
ton arton hêmôn ton epiousion dos hêmin sêmeron [Ton arton imon ton epioosion dos imin simeron ]
the bread of substance/existence give us daily

Super substantial bread

usia means substance

epiusia means on top of or greater than the substance

[Transliteration/Translation](https://ministrants.com/lords-prayer/the-lords-prayer-in-greek-classical-modern-versions/)

The principle concern of this Gospel is to provide testimony to the enduring presence of Christ in the Eucharist—a presence which is the fulfillment of the ancient temple of Israel. The ark of the covenant contained the law of God and the manna from heaven, and was surrounded by the mysterious “showbread” or “bread of the presence.” Now, in the true tabernacles found in our churches, we find the living law, the true manna, and the definitive bread of the presence

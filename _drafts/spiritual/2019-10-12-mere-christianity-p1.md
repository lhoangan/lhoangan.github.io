I have to admit that this is the first time ever in my life that I've done a book
summary, regardless of English or Vietnamese. And this is also, to my best recall,
the first book and I've read back-to-back (I don't really count novels or comics,
for feeling of their lack of wisdom). Although feeling the urge to read books, I
have never found a good time to just sit down, pick up a book and start reading.
Most of the time I got lost in all sorts of multimedia, movies, news, social media,
etc., and when it comes to reading, textbooks, scientific papers, and technical
articles take all the priority. But I always know that I have to do it, reading
can be so benefitial than I can image: all accomplished people, doesn't matter
if they're ordinary people that I meet everyday, or extraordinary that only appear
on the news and rumors, they all read. To me, reading can bring not only knowledge
or wisdom, but also strengthen my English skill, reading speed, memory, patience
and perseverance. Long story short, after all the efforts, I nailed it (:yeah!).

At the same time, I was also overwhelmed because of all the information and lessons
and knowledge in it. That's why this book summary. Surely it's not the best
summary every of the book, for it's a classic and well-known one, but it's the
best of my effort about the best points that I found important or simply amused
about in the book.

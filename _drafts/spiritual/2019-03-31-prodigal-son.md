The reading today is about the prodigal son. It is a well-known story, and I've
heard several homilies on the topics, covering several (if not to say all) aspects
of the parable.

Yet it's still interesting to hear it again.

This part touched me deeply today.

When the second son comes back, the Father saw him first when he's still from 
afar: the Father respects the decision of the son so he let him goes but always
longs for him, making him always on guard and unceasingly watch out for him.

The reunite moment is touching: the son coming back smelly and all the weary
clothes, and dirty, but the Father first hold him in his arm and kiss him tenderly.
Despite all the speech that the son prepared, he just, as to say, doesn't give 
a damn, the next thing you find him to say is ordering the people to dress and
restore the son's position, a son. It feels like a magnifique happy-ending in
fairy tales, or a drama movie.

The first son lives with the Father but doesn't stay in his heart: he doesn't
know how and what the father think. Likewise, he stay with the father, but 
the father doesn't have a place in his heart: he obey him but out of order and
respect: "all these years I've been slaved to you". This reminds me, that
some times we, the good people, try to live within the law of God, but doesn't
feel love. Jesus said it doesn't matter if you keep the law, if you don't have
love that's nothing. the law of god is to protect us and keep us safe in his love
and so we are free to love him, not to enslave us, Are we living like a slave or
a son?

It also reminds me that some times it's good to behave like a child, to some times
court fondling/coddle from God who is our father. TO say to him like Martha says:
"You beloved is sick", 'You beloved son is needing you now"

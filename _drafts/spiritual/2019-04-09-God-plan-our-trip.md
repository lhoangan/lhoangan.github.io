It was one [date], I woke up to the call of a friend for an unfamiliar friend
who got robbed in Paris. Initially thought is a polite refusion. Honestly, I'm
not a type of socialization and feeling troubling to meet up with new people.

I'm pondering about the matter throughout the breakfast. Did I try to put myself
in the victim friend's shoese? (reflect).

I remember I told myself that I did that for God's sake.

It doesn't mean too much troubles, just extra works, basically I didn't lose anything
just a little extra effort to go a little different direction from normal route 
from work home. I didn't have trouble at work, and didn't have to sacrify anything.

2 year later, she became so helpful when I was planning the trip for my parents..

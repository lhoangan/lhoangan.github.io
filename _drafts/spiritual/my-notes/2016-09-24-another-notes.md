Yet another spiritual note of me
 

I am still struggling in choosing the appropriate language for my notes. Vietnamese seems to be the best language in terms of expressing and feeling, while in some case it is better for me to say out loud in English, not because I don’t know how to say them in Vietnamese but because it feels so cheesy that way. Another reason for my struggles is I’d like to practice English, writing is a good way, especially to express my feeling in some unconventional way. However, in this work, I still think VNese would be a better choice, because of its importance with respect to my life and and my faith, which far overcome the need of practicing English. I’ll make it that way, so that I’ll have to write more in the future, in English then, to have it practiced.

Today's gospel is on [Mark 8:14-21](http://www.usccb.org/bible/readings/bible/mark/8:14)
telling the story that the disciples were worried because they forgot to bring breads,
and Jesus reminded them about his miracles of breaking five loaves for five thousand.

Very often we see in the bible how the disciples lose their faith while living
in close proximity to Jesus, with countless of miracles performed in front of
their eyes, still they can lose faith in just a blink of an eye. Very often,
we heard aethiests argue to see the true form of God to believe, or found
ourselves become hard to believe and seek out to see a miracles from God so that
we can be sure that He is there and not some sort of nice concept being nicely
made up in human history. These has already been an issue from the Jewish people.
Remember their demanding of Jesus coming down from the cross and they will believe?
I believe that each one of us has at least once pondered why Jesus did not do that,
or what would have happened if he'd done so.

What the Lord tells us is in the parable of the rich man and Lazarus [Luke 16:19-31](http://www.usccb.org/bible/luke/16:19), 

Then Abraham said, "If they will not listen to Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded if someone should rise from the dead." [Luke 16:31](http://www.usccb.org/bible/luke/16:31)

This tells us one hard-to-accept fact that: miracles themselves could contribute
to some extent to our faith,  by strengthening our faith, but they cannot be the source
of our faith. In other words, miracles cannot make us believe. We human beings are
weak, and easy to lose faith, and even when we can see all sort of miracles.

The true source of our faith must come from God Himself only, and faith is nothing
but God's Grace. Without His helps and wills, we can never have faith, even when 
we think we do.

I've come across many miracles in mylife, all sort of events with very low odd to
happen, and at least once in my life, I've felt the strong of faith.
But once troubles and challenges come, it becomes really hard to keep the faith,
when things got really intense, we can easy doubt the presence of God, and all
sorts of terrible things just come up into your mind, even thinking that
the things that you once believed miracles become some silly coincidences.

So that's that, miracles are not all, they are there to help us strengthen our
believes and being sent by God will when He thinks they'll be of help. But don't
constantly seek for miracles and only believe when there's one, but seek for God's
grace through prayers, Holy Communion, attending Masses. Only there can you meet
Him in person, only there can you receive Him truly in your soul, and only there
can you be strengthened in your faith.

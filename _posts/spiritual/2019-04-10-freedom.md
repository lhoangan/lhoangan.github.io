What is freedom? Freedom is today what I want

Another point of views: freedom is to do what is right?

Can I always do what is right?  How easy is that?

Not always. If it means to lose something beneficial, can I still do it? If it
means to lose my life? 

Right now, I am feeling so intense and stressed because I am chasing the goal
and try to produce a paper in 3 weeks without experience (on the topic, on the tools)
why? for no reasonable reason: it's a small conference and it's easy to publish.
But if it's done rushly like it, can it be good enough to publish. I stil don't know.

But the trade-off is I'm constantly stressed and worried and concerned. Yet the final
goal is to stay happy, and to learn new knowledge, and train my skill. But I completely
forget that, all what I think and imagine is how good I can be when I publish the paper.
That's an illusion.

But having all that, I found it extremely difficult to withdraw myself from that illusion
and focus on the right thing: to learn and exploring and enjoying. I am completed slave
of my illusion. That could further make me irritated and become annoyed and demanding
and may mistreat myself and my friends. So I no longer have freedom. I am enslaved by 
this sinful thought of power?

In the reading to day, the guys was total free to choose who to serve, even it means
dead. But with faith they say "if our Lord can recuse us, he will, if he can't we still won't"
It shows that they are not sure that they will be rescued, but they are free to choose what
they know is right.

in the Gospel, Jesus said who stays in me are free from sin

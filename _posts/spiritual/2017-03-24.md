Today is a wonderful day. I don't know if it was because I have had a very smooth
and fun day or if it was that I have finished a substantial part and get it run
fine, but the subsequence is I am feeling good. I am feeling good even in the
midst of the guilt of leaving the office before the professor does, the guilt of
leaving him do his job without trying to stay close and help him correct and
giving comments.  

It was a good day: the weather is good, it was sunny and warm with a peaceful
blue sky and a little white clouds. We finished our ASCI course assignment,
ended at the third rank in the course challenge, share and copy the results to
each other so that all can finish their assignment. Today is the last day of CV1
lab, and we had to prepare the materials (including exam sheets, questions,
surveys) to proctor the exam on the next coming Monday. We talked about our
experience in the course and once it is finished, it is the lift of the feeling:
when the pressure is off, I am getting back the motivation to start preparing for
a the next coming session of it. A lots of idea and plans have been erected
inside my head, from the experience of the last course, I now want to do so many
things to improve course quality, and foremost, my knowledge, my understanding
and my experience in the course. I linked it with my research, I dreamt about a
future, when I can be a professor (or at least an expert, or maybe just a teacher
or a knowledgeable guy in the field). I like to work more to know more about this
low level Computer Vision, and from that my dream grew. Today, my professor was
fun, he smiled a lot, and not yelling (or teasing me, and I was glad for it).
He was rushing to finish the question sheets for the upcoming final exam before
he can leave for his vacation starting on the following day, and last for a week.
I am also glad and feel relief for I now still have one week to finish my
commitment, and one extra (with lots of stuff to do, actually) before facing him. 

In the midst of all the dreaming, and relief and happiness, I found the source
of all of it. Had it been me who did all of this? Had it been me who brought me
to this position and achieved all of this? Had it been me who bring this feeling
to me? I don't think so. There are many things that I can take account for: I
can claim the effort, time and health that I spent on working on Blender; I can
claim the programming skill that I have leant and trained since my college time;
I can claim my persistence in pursuing a goal and good choice when choosing to
learn Linux and now having the ability to help other colleagues in the office,
thus creating a nice and fun atmosphere.  

However, was it me who had a very beginning idea to learn Linux? No, I have
tried so many time, but failed, and I was even failed in effort of keeping
pursuing it? I once gave up and didn't want to learn Linux, because of lacking
of motivation, lacking of good tutorials and safe environments to get myself
familiar and thus cannot learn it till these very days. If it wasn't because of
this environment / job that requires coding in Linux (caffe) and if it wasn't
because there is Dennis, and if it wasn't because there is a gap time after my
master, I wouldn't have a chance to getting myself familiar with it, and then
developed it. If it wasn't because my friends (Nguyen, Minh, Sua) has motivated
my (in terms of initiating it and dare me to do it and compete in skillfulness),
I wouldn't have done it. And if you think that because I am open-minded to start
admit myself and willing to learn from others, forgive me to laugh at your face.
I am not: I am shy and afraid so show my stupidity (at least to the public or
other people), I have been a guy who try to show off and prove myself in front
of the other people. This is bad. If there wasn't a time when I was abandoned by
my "close" friends, and so I had to open my mind and heart to live and play with
other "low-level" friends, I wouldn't have this little openness, thanks to which
I can now know a bit (more than some others). And think of it, if it wasn't
because of Nguyen and his method, and because of my failures in total and his
achievement during the Master time, I wouldn't have a mind to learn from him,
and thus no note-taking whatsoever, which means no learning (from errors) at all,
and thus, no skill! And if you think more of it, even with this "much" skill that
I am having right now, if I was put in the middle of the QUVA or ISIS lab, I
would have died of shame or at least felt so much timid to actually admitted or
lost confidence, and thus no "good atmosphere" at all. You have to see that thanks
to this wonderful environment that seem so fit me, the environment that I was put
to was sufficient and suitable for me (with all the friends who starts at around
the same level (or at least having similar skills) as me, and having their own
problems and hobbies, all of that makes this day) 

If I  didn't have somehow good health (or at least recovered from a more terrible
state to this health) I wouldn't have handled and persisted enough to learn all
of it. If it wasn't because of the meeting (which seemed to be so pointless,
with Ruth and very subtle details that I accidentally caught from Nikolaus'
presentation of Blender, and come to think of it, the 3D-Graphic course from
EURECOM has contributed to much in the sense of encouraging me to use Blender).
All the tutorials that I accidentally came across while trying to learn Blender,
and this problem of garden coincidently includes some how good notice from the
community that there are a good number of tutorials on trees, and ivy, and
terrains, and nature aspect, and sky and rocks. Come to think of it, I didn't
think of how much knowledge of these I would need when starting with Blender,
I had thought to try to create couples of few trees, and sky and grass and
that's all. But without ivy and bush and topiary, none of the other matters!!
Who was it to claim? Not me 

It was a feeling, a very strong feeling of grateful from the weekly Lenten
Stations of the Cross, this Friday evening that I got. It was grateful, grateful
because of this beautiful path that I have been walking though, the failure
become the success, the weakness becomes the strong points. That is “'My grace
is sufficient for you, for power is made perfect in weakness.” I will rather
boast most gladly of my weaknesses, in order that the power of Christ may dwell
with me" (2Cor 12:9) and "Therefore, I am content with weaknesses, insults,
hardships, persecutions, and constraints, for the sake of Christ; for when I am
weak, then I am strong." (2Cor 12:10)

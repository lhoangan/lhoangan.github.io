Dear my friend,

Today I met a common friend of us, and he handed me this amazing gift of you.
It made me laughed. It was funny because the guy who had no idea that I didn't
ask you for it, so he didn't say who sent it, yet right at the moment I saw it,
I knew right away it was you.

I did really appreciate it and how you try to care for me. But let's face it,
it can do me nothing, can't it? Yet, somehow it's not very surprise to me.
Through out the two years that we stayed together, we've been through quite
some scenarios, and in most cases, you seemed to be always the guy who play it
cool. I can see how you do care for the people around you, but somehow you just
never show it, and eventually you show it up with a funny cheesy thing.

I can never understand that. And that's how our ideology is different.o

Everyone has their own problem, and better than anyone that they know what's
the best from them at each moment. Bystanders could sometimes be helpful too, if
we have experienced the same thing or have expertised knowledge. Yet that does
not happen very often. Thus, I believe that much better than a solution (and of 
course always inferior to a workable solution), the attitude that we care for
someone could be a good gift for them. It is a little strange for me that, 
after the 2 years with you, you made me believe that for you may be it's a
workable solution or nothing at all.

You did't want me to ask when you were in trouble, you didn't want to share
anything even though I tried to care.

But let's face it, nothing a bystander can do to get you out of a situation,
a bad feeling, and mental problem. But a word of caring, a phrase of encouragement
or even better sharing experience, sharing the problem could at least on mental
level get people up.

But doesn't it mean that we just thrown at each other cheese, empty, repetitve
phrases? Do we just simply throw those "you can do it", "I believe in you", or
"hold it together" and be done with it? What do you think?

Of course not, not much different from a pack of curcumin sent across continent
3 days after the sick, do those phrases can do any good to a friend in sickness,
yet we must be admit they're light and cheap and can be sent instantly.

Does the cheap price debunk diseffect them somehow? Could be, but what if you
say it and mean it. Meaning it is something that not so many people can do, as
well as to which extent they really mean it.

If you really mean to care for someone, like really mean it, like mean it with
your heart, you'd know that you'll try all the things to get the other the best.
If you always mean it, you'll find yourself in situations of helplessness, you'll
see that you yourself cannot do everything. About this point, a lot of people
can easily agreee that human being cannot do everything. Yet very few of them
really experience that, and even fewer experience them to a regular basis. I
believe that those who experiencing them at daily or hourly basis will understand
that there's a power above all thing and only by clinging on to the power that
you know everything will be just fine.o

This remind me a story that I heard just in a few months time, about a French
student who studies in Ireland and heard about the incident of Notre Dame de
Paris was on fire. He said "I saw it on TV and desperately want to do something
to help. But I didn't know what to do. It's too far away" You know what I
thought at this moment? Well, even the parisiens who live and stay right in Paris
can do nothing. Nothing physically. The problem is we'll all connected in a 
trivial way in time and space that we believe that that is the only way to connect
us. We are absolute alienated to other way of connection that we thought only
through time and space can we connect to one other. I believe that there're other
ways as well. By praying, and entrusting everything to the Lord God, we can rest
assure that nothing is going in vain, and that the fires can be put down, sickness
can be cured, our people we love can be seen again. By meaning love to one another
we draw ourselves closer to God, in prayers and in doing good for one another,
thus making us become truly helpful in any situation.

Inde my case, let's face it. You're now a thousand miles away from mekk

Though it might occur subtle to him as he thought I'd asked you about it, that
split of a second did poke my heart, and I literally jumped for that.

Thank you very much. I appreciate that.

Of all people that know you, may be I am the only one think that we can never
meet again, and by the time I am writing this line, I am only a bus from Amsterdam
to Frankfurt, forseeing that we will meet again.

Again, despite all of the differences between us, ideology, life style, belief,
once again that single common friend is the place of our reunion. How funny is it?

Honestly speaking, after we departed from our graduation, I did not want to see
you again because I found that through out the 2 years knowing each other we
had been nothing but the goal for each other. A goal is good to bring each other
up but in our case, that goal is near to unreachable, and thus one remains no
better than an envy for the other.

That's bad, as an apparently good idea suddenly becomes an obstacle.
And we departed, I thought you would never 


You know it's kinda funny to think 

I am touched to receive your gift, because I see how you care for me.

But let's face it, your gift arrives late, and it's difficult for me to use, render it rather useless.

When I first saw your gift, I laughed, both for how funny it is, and how happy I am.

It's pretty useless. But I know how deeply you care for me.

With this distance, there's nothing you can do for me. We can talk, I can tell you
what's happening to me. Although you can't understand fully, can't feel it if
you're not experiencing it. But for the sake of the story, let say I can somehow
convey the whole information with all its sentiment to you. What can you do?
Nothing! You can talk to me and to some extent comfort me, but you can do further than that.


Pretty much like how I'm dead to you (reflect more to this relationship)

In my faith, I believe you can do more than that. Because we're all connect with
the Lord, our God, and He's our Father, and He's every where, and He can do basically
everything. So if you feel the urge to do something for me, say a prayer and really mean it.
If you don't believe in God, it's fine. But again, if you really do care for me, just say it.
but your heart and love into it, if you do it for the sake of me, that's all it needs.
Faith is a grace, and us alone can not achieve it, but a step of love is a step toward God,
and once you navigate toward him. He'll come to you. I believe one day you'll find him.

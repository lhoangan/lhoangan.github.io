Dear my friend,

I am touched to receive your gift, because I see how you care for me.

But let's face it, your gift arrives late, and it's difficult for me to use, render it rather useless.

When I first saw your gift, I laughed, both for how funny it is, and how happy I am.

It's pretty useless. But I know how deeply you care for me.

With this distance, there's nothing you can do for me. We can talk, I can tell you
what's happening to me. Although you can't understand fully, can't feel it if
you're not experiencing it. But for the sake of the story, let say I can somehow
convey the whole information with all its sentiment to you. What can you do?
Nothing! You can talk to me and to some extent comfort me, but you can do further than that.


Pretty much like how I'm dead to you (reflect more to this relationship)

In my faith, I believe you can do more than that. Because we're all connect with
the Lord, our God, and He's our Father, and He's every where, and He can do basically
everything. So if you feel the urge to do something for me, say a prayer and really mean it.
If you don't believe in God, it's fine. But again, if you really do care for me, just say it.
but your heart and love into it, if you do it for the sake of me, that's all it needs.
Faith is a grace, and us alone can not achieve it, but a step of love is a step toward God,
and once you navigate toward him. He'll come to you. I believe one day you'll find him.

What's going on when we pray?

What is prayer, metaphysically speaking, and how does it work?

Prayer is not to change God's mind, God is unchanging, uncondition absolutely.

Prayer is not meant to change God's mind, inform him something He didn't know or
persuade Him.

Few aspects:

- Consistency in praying (taught by Jesus in []()) : through consistent praying
we get closer to God

- Grant after prayer: think of it this way, it's been always God's intention to
give us that, He just need us to get real into because through consistent praying
we get closer to Him, or through patient and praying we get ourselves (or rather
He helps us get ourselves) ready for the gift. It could be at the time of praying
we didn't need it (maybe it's just us who want it, but we didn't truly need it)

- Something ungranted: Maybe it's yet to the time that we would need it, maybe
we just want it and are not ready for it, maybe that's something really harmful
and God does not want us to have

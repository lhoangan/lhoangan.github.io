MEMORIAL OF SAINT ANDREW DUNG-LAC, PRIEST, AND COMPANIONS, MARTYRS

LUKE 21:5-11

Friends, in today’s Gospel, Jesus teaches us what to expect in the times between his first and second coming. 

He warns against false messiahs: "Many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he’ and ‘The time has come.’ Do not follow them!" One of the marks of the in-between times is that false messiahs will arise, those who claim to represent God and the purposes of God. Even a casual survey of the last two thousand years reveals the validity of this observation. Various political leaders, social reformers, dictators, gurus, and artists have claimed an ultimate authority. We have seen a plethora of such figures in very recent times. They are all representatives of the old world, even as they claim to represent the new. 

Jesus also alerts us to expect international wars: "When you hear of wars and insurrections, do not be terrified. . . . Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom." When an animal is threatened and wounded, he will fight even more fiercely. There is a terrible instability to the sinful world—lashing out, judging, attacking, bluffing, conquering and being conquered. That is simply the way of the nations in the grip of sin.

WEDNESDAY OF THE SECOND WEEK OF ADVENT

MATTHEW 11:28-30

Friends, in today’s Gospel, Jesus says, "Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am meek and humble of heart; and you will find rest for yourselves. For my yoke is easy, and my burden light."

Anthony de Mello proposed the following parable to describe us prideful souls. A group of people sit on a bus that is passing through the most glorious countryside, but they have the shades pulled down on all the windows and are bickering about who gets the front seat on the bus. This is the burden of pride: preferring the narrow and stuffy confines of the self to the beauty that is effortlessly available all around.

This, of course, is why Jesus can say, "My yoke is easy, and my burden light." What the Lord proposes is not a freedom from suffering but, what is much more important, a freedom from the self.

Reflect: Being yoked to Christ means we turn all control of our lives over to him. Can you do that? Do you want to?

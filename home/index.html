Introduction

About Me

My name is Hoang-An Le (with full diacritics in Vietnamese, Lê Hoàng Ân). I was born in Ho Chi Minh city, Vietnam.

Starting from April 2016, I am a PhD student at Computer Vision lab, University of Amsterdam in the Netherlands. I am working with Professor Theo Gevers on the project TrimBot2020, funded by EUHorizon. My thesis topic focuses on 3D semantic segmentation.

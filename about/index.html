Introduction

About Me

Greetings! My name is Hoàng-Ân Lê. I am from Ho Chi Minh city, Vietnam.

Starting from April 2016, I am a doctoral candidate at Computer Vision lab, University of Amsterdam in the Netherlands. I am working with Professor Theo Gevers on the project TrimBot2020, funded by EUHorizon, in which I was interviewed for the Human Face of Robotics. My thesis topic is on multi-modality Computer Vision.

I have been a regular reviewer for several high-ranking conferences and journals, including CVPR (IEEE/CVF), BMVC (BMVA), WACV (IEEE/CVF), ACCV (AFCV), JSTARS (IEEE)

My CV can be found here , last updated in October 2020.
